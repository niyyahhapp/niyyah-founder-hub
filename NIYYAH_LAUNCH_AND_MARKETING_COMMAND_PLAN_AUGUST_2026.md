# NIYYAH Launch and Marketing Command Plan

Updated: August 13, 2026

## Launch Position

NIYYAH is a private, source-aware Qur'an learning companion for understanding one ayah at a time with people you trust.

The launch message is not "another Qur'an reader" and not "an AI Qur'an app."

The launch message is:

**Understand the Qur'an together.**

## The Five Features To Market Hardest

### 1. Private Qur'an Circles

This is NIYYAH's signature feature.

- Create a Circle for family, friends, an MSA, or a classroom.
- Join with an invite code or QR code.
- Read, reflect, and check in together.
- No public feed, followers, or popularity contest.
- Shared consistency without exposing private reflections.

Marketing hook: **"Your group chat talks all day. What if one corner of it brought you back to the Qur'an?"**

### 2. One Ayah At A Time

- Focused Arabic and translation.
- Verified recitation inside the app.
- Clear path from reading to understanding and reflection.
- Easy return to the current ayah.

Marketing hook: **"You do not need an hour. Begin with one ayah."**

### 3. Trust and Source Transparency

- Qur'an text is never generated or rewritten by AI.
- Verified reciter audio, not generic text-to-speech.
- Attributed source tafsir only where it has actually been added.
- Missing source tafsir is omitted instead of invented.
- Sources & Trust and content-reporting tools are visible.

Marketing hook: **"When a verified source is not available, NIYYAH leaves it out instead of guessing."**

### 4. Private Reflection Toolkit

- Guided reflection prompts.
- Personal journal and dua space.
- Saved vocabulary.
- No advertising against private religious activity.

Marketing hook: **"Read it. Understand it. Let it change something."**

### 5. Gentle Progress

- Learning journeys and quick quizzes.
- Progress without shame.
- Return after a missed day without losing everything.
- Circle check-ins create social accountability without public pressure.

Marketing hook: **"Consistency without guilt."**

## What Makes The Free Beta Remarkable

Lead with what users receive, not with what competitors charge.

- No ads.
- No subscription screen.
- Reading, translation, verified recitation, available source tafsir, reflection, journal, vocabulary, quizzes, journeys, progress, and private Circles are free.
- No public feed.
- No selling private reflections.

Never say every ayah has source tafsir. Never imply the app replaces scholars or teachers.

## Audience Plan

### First Audience

- Muslim students and young adults.
- Muslim women who want a calm, private learning space.
- MSA leaders and Islamic-school teachers.
- Families and friend groups who want a shared Qur'an routine.
- Qur'an-study and convert-support groups.

### African and African-Diaspora Launch

Do not market to "Africa" as one audience. Launch by language and community.

For the English beta, prioritize English-speaking Muslim communities in Canada, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, and their diaspora communities. Use local Muslim student creators, Muslim women creators, MSAs, school groups, and trusted community educators.

Do not advertise in French, Hausa, Swahili, Somali, or Arabic until the app and campaign copy are properly localized and reviewed in that language.

## Channel Strategy

### TikTok and Instagram Reels

Post native, founder-led videos rather than polished generic ads.

Core formats:

1. Twenty-second app demonstration.
2. Founder story: why NIYYAH was built.
3. "One ayah with me" screen recording.
4. Create a Circle and invite one friend.
5. A trust explainer showing Sources & Trust.
6. A before/after: scattered notes versus one guided flow.
7. A response to a real beta-tester question.

### Instagram Carousels

Use five to seven slides with one idea per slide.

1. "Why understanding the Qur'an can feel overwhelming."
2. "What NIYYAH does differently."
3. "How private Circles work."
4. "What happens when source tafsir is unavailable."
5. "Everything included in the free beta."

### WhatsApp and Community Sharing

- Make one clean launch poster and one 15-second vertical demo.
- Give MSA leaders a short message plus the App Store link.
- Give teachers a separate classroom-focused message.
- Give users a ready-made Circle invitation message.
- Ask for one specific action: install, create a Circle, or invite two people.

### Reddit

- Post only where self-promotion is allowed.
- Lead with the problem and request feedback, not an advertisement.
- Be transparent that the founder built the app.
- Never mass-post identical copy across communities.

### X / Twitter

- One short founder build thread.
- Daily product clips or screenshots.
- Reply thoughtfully to Qur'an-learning and Muslim-product conversations.
- Avoid engagement bait and religious controversy.

## Launch Content Schedule

### Day Before Release

- Founder teaser: "I built the Qur'an app I needed."
- Carousel: why NIYYAH exists.
- Story poll: "Who would be in your Qur'an Circle?"
- Send early access to ten trusted testers.

### Release Day

- Main 20-second launch reel.
- App Store screenshots carousel.
- Founder story video.
- MSA/community message.
- X launch thread.
- Ask every tester to create one Circle and invite one person.

### Days 2-7

- One real feature demonstration daily.
- One trust/source post.
- One founder progress update.
- One user question answered on video.
- One Circle challenge prompt.

Do not buy followers. Do not automate spam. Do not make unverifiable religious, privacy, adoption, or competitor claims.

## The Metrics That Matter

- App Store product-page conversion.
- Install to onboarding completion.
- First ayah opened.
- First audio play.
- First reflection saved.
- Circle created or joined.
- Invite accepted.
- Day-3 and day-7 return.

Follower count is secondary.
