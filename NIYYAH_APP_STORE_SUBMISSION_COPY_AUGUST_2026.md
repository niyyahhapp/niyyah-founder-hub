## Description

NIYYAH is a calm Qur'an learning companion for Muslims who want to understand one ayah at a time and stay connected with people they trust.

The app guides you one ayah at a time so you can slow down, read clearly, understand the meaning, and reflect with intention.

With NIYYAH, you can:

- Read Qur'an ayahs in a focused, distraction-free screen.
- View translation and available source tafsir.
- Begin surahs with context and key lessons where reviewed content is available.
- Reflect through guided prompts.
- Save journal entries and personal duas.
- Track completed ayahs and memorization progress.
- Save favorite ayahs and vocabulary words for later review.
- Follow gentle learning journeys and quizzes.
- Create or join private Circles for families, friends, MSAs, and classrooms.
- Use shared check-ins without a public feed or public follower counts.

The NIYYAH beta is free and contains no advertising.

NIYYAH is not a fatwa app and does not replace qualified scholars or teachers. It is a learning and reflection companion designed to make Qur'an understanding feel calmer, clearer, and more approachable.

## App Review Notes

NIYYAH is a free Qur'an learning and reflection app. This version contains no paid features, purchase prompts, advertising, or subscription flow. Draft subscriptions in App Store Connect are not part of this submission.

The app displays Qur'an Arabic text from bundled verified data and displays source tafsir only where attributed source material has been added. It does not generate tafsir, fatwas, or religious rulings. Private reflections and duas stay on the user's device. Optional account sign-in is used for private Circle creation, joining, membership, and check-in sync.

An account is not required to review the core reading, listening, understanding, reflection, journal, vocabulary, progress, or learning-journey experience. Sign-in is optional and used for private Circles. Account deletion is available from the profile/account screen after sign-in. Circle content is invite-only; there is no public feed, anonymous chat, or public posting.

## App Privacy Answers Draft

Data collected:

- Email Address: only if account login or waitlist features are active.
- User ID: only if account login/backend sync is active.
- Name: the optional Circle display name is synced when the user uses account-backed Circles.
- Other User Content: Circle name/purpose, membership, and check-in status are synced only when the user chooses account-backed Circles.
- Private reflections, duas, vocabulary, and reading progress stay on the device in this build and are not collected by NIYYAH's backend.

Data not used for tracking:

- NIYYAH does not sell personal data.
- NIYYAH does not use user reflections for advertising tracking.
- NIYYAH should not send journal text, dua text, private reflections, Qur'an notes, Arabic text, translation text, or private search queries to analytics or purchase systems.
