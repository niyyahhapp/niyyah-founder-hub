# NIYYAH 30-Day Posting Schedule

This campaign contains 90 carousel posts: three posts per day for 30 days.

Primary link: https://niyyahhapp.github.io/niyyah-app/

## Daily Rhythm

- Morning: first carousel on Instagram and TikTok Photo Mode.
- Afternoon: second carousel on TikTok Photo Mode, Threads, X, LinkedIn, or another NIYYAH account.
- Evening: third carousel as Stories, a second-platform post, or a direct community CTA.
- Aim for three content touches across channels; do not flood one Instagram grid if quality or reach drops.

## Day 1

NIYYAH is a calm Qur'an learning companion built for reading, understanding, reflection, and private Circles. The beta is free. Link in bio.

### Post 2: Read less. Understand more.

You do not need to finish a whole surah today. Start with one ayah, understand what you can, and return gently.

### Post 3: Free means free.

The core Qur'an learning experience is free in NIYYAH's beta. No guilt, no fake urgency, and no paywall around understanding the Qur'an.

### Post 3: Listen without leaving the app.

## Day 4

### Post 1: For a Qur'an app, trust comes first.

A Qur'an app should tell you where its content comes from. NIYYAH keeps source material and educational tools clearly separated.

Qur'an recitation in NIYYAH uses verified reciter audio, never a generic AI voice.

## Day 27

### Post 1: The Qur'an text is not generated.

Sacred text should come from an established dataset. NIYYAH keeps Qur'an text separate from educational tools and never asks AI to rewrite it. Read the source commitment.

### Post 2: Translation needs attribution too.

The English wording is a translation, not the Qur'an itself. NIYYAH identifies translation sources so users can understand what they are reading. Check the attribution.

### Post 3: Source tafsir: only where available.

Coverage is expanding carefully, not pretending to be complete. If an attributed source passage has not been added, the app leaves the source section out. Transparency over appearance.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not claim every ayah has source tafsir. Say attributed source tafsir where available.
- Do not call NIYYAH an AI-powered Qur'an app.
- Do not create fake testimonials, ratings, download counts, or urgency.
- Do not attack another Muslim app or make unverified ownership claims.
- Do not expose private reflections, duas, journals, invite codes, or Circle activity.
- Creators must use required gifted or paid partnership disclosures.
- Do not localize campaign copy until a qualified language reviewer approves it.
